How it works

A simple weekly ritual.

Not another app to manage.

About the maker

Built by someone who's been through it.

After 20+ years in software, my company was restructured. New ownership, several rounds of cost cuts, a push toward automation. I watched the writing on the wall — and I wasn't the only one.

I built this tool for myself while navigating this huge shift in the industry. I've been using it every day since. At some point I started asking myself why I hadn't shared it yet.

This won't guarantee your job. But it will end the feeling of falling behind — and that's worth something.
