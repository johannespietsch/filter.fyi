The web app is one entry point. The longer-term shape is the same engine reachable from wherever you save things — your chat app, your terminal, your editor's AI.

A short overview of each, with anchors so you can deep-link to a section.

#telegram #cli #mcp

Telegram#

early access

Forward a link to the filter.fyi bot and get the same verdict you'd get on the web — without leaving the chat app. The bot also keeps the saved-for-you library in sync with your account.

  • One bot, your account on both sides.
  • Works on URLs, forwarded messages, and shared posts.
  • Linking happens from your signed-in dashboard, not by chatting the bot first.

CLI#

planned

A terminal client for the same flow — pipe a URL in, get a verdict and the structured analysis back. Useful for scripts, shell pipelines, and people who live in tmux.

Shape we're targeting: filter <url> for one-shot, filter saved for the library, JSON output behind --json.

MCP#

planned

Model Context Protocol server so your AI assistant (Claude Code, the API, IDE agents) can call filter.fyi directly. Ask your assistant to triage a list of links and it can hand them off here without you copy-pasting.

Authenticates against the same account as the web and Telegram, so saved items show up in one library.
