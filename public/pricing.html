Questions you'd probably ask

Why isn't it all free?
Every read costs us real model inference — there are no ads and your attention is not the product. Free stays genuinely useful (3 reads a day, full verdicts and briefs); Pro funds the compounding parts: monitoring, memory, and the digest.
Why not "just use ChatGPT"?
You can paste a link into a chat — and get a summary you still have to read and judge. filter.fyi is opinionated where a chat is neutral: a verdict against your lens, a memory of everything you've filtered, monitoring that runs while you don't, and hand-off briefs designed to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor. We're not your chat's competitor — we're its front door.
What exactly is a "founding member"?
The first cohort of paying users. You get Pro at $10/mo for as long as you stay subscribed (half the sticker price, forever), early access to every new surface (MCP, digest, monitoring), and a direct line to the founder for feature requests. In exchange, you put up with the occasional rough edge and tell us about it.
Can I get my data out?
Yes — one-click JSON export of everything (your library, lens, and shortlist) from your account page, and account deletion is just as easy. See privacy.
Do verdicts ever get it wrong?
Yes, sometimes — they're AI-generated and we say so on every result. That's why each verdict shows what it's based on ("what we read") and why next steps are suggestions with briefs you review, not actions we take for you.
