Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Last updated 20 May 2026

Plain-language summary of how filter.fyi handles your data. We collect as little as we can, don't sell anything, and use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we are

filter.fyi ("we", "the service") is operated by Johannes, based in the Netherlands. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: hello@filter.fyi.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist

  • Your email address, and the optional challenge answers you tick.

If you use the service

  • The URLs and content you submit for analysis, and the analysis we generate from them.
  • If you create an account: your email address and your saved items.
  • Limited technical data for security and rate-limiting: your IP address and a randomly generated anonymous ID stored in a cookie. These are kept short-term.

We don't ask for, or want, sensitive personal data. Please don't submit content you wouldn't want processed by an automated AI system.

How we use it & legal bases

PurposeLegal basis (GDPR)
Provide the service — analyse what you submit, save your libraryPerformance of a contract
Send transactional email (sign-in links, account messages)Performance of a contract
Prevent abuse and keep the service available (rate-limiting)Legitimate interest
Email you about early access if you joined the waitlistConsent

You can withdraw waitlist consent at any time by emailing us — see "Your rights".

Who processes your data

We use a small number of trusted providers ("processors") to run the service. We don't sell your data and we don't share it for advertising.

ProviderRole
CloudflareWebsite hosting and edge storage
Fly.ioBackend hosting (EU region), database
Anthropic (or OpenAI)The AI model that analyses submitted content
ResendSending transactional & waitlist email

Content you submit is sent to our AI provider to generate a verdict. Our providers process this on our behalf and, on their standard API terms, do not use it to train their models. Some providers are based in the US; transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

We use only what's needed to run the service — no advertising or analytics trackers.

  • fyi_session — keeps you signed in (strictly necessary).
  • fyi_anon — a random ID used for fair-use rate-limiting before you sign in.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist email — until early access ends or you ask us to remove it.
  • Account & saved items — until you delete the item or your account.
  • Rate-limit data (IP, anon ID) — short-lived; cleared on a rolling daily basis.
  • Diagnostic error logs — about 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and object to or restrict certain processing.

  • Delete your account & data or export your data — from your library page once signed in, or by emailing us.
  • Waitlist removal — email hello@filter.fyi and we'll delete your entry.

If you're in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the date at the top. Material changes affecting you will be communicated by email where appropriate.
